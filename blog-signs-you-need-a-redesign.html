7 Signs Your Business Needs a Website Redesign

Oct 2026 · 4 min read

Is your website quietly costing you customers? Here are seven warning signs that it is time for a refresh.

Your website is often the first impression a customer has of your business. If it looks outdated or is hard to use, visitors leave — usually for a competitor. Here are seven signs it is time for a redesign.

  1. It does not work well on mobile phones. Most visitors now browse on a phone; a site that is not responsive loses them instantly.
  2. It loads slowly. Every extra second of loading time increases the chance a visitor gives up.
  3. It looks outdated. Old fonts, cluttered layouts and low-quality images make a business look out of touch.
  4. Visitors do not convert. If people visit but rarely call, buy or fill in a form, your design may be getting in the way.
  5. You cannot update it yourself. A modern CMS lets you edit text, images and products without a developer.
  6. It does not reflect your brand. If your business has grown or rebranded, your website should too.
  7. It ranks poorly on Google. Poor structure, slow speed and missing SEO basics hold back your visibility.

What a redesign should deliver

A good redesign is not just a new coat of paint. It should make your site faster, easier to use, mobile-friendly and focused on turning visitors into customers — while keeping the SEO value you have already built.

If you recognise two or more of these signs, talk to our team about a redesign that is built to convert.
