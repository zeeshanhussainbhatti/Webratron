FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger projects such as e-commerce stores or custom web apps usually take 4–10 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Our packages start from $999 for a professional starter website. Every business is different, so we provide a free, detailed quote based on your exact requirements — no hidden costs.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Yes. Every site we build is fully responsive, fast-loading and follows SEO best practices out of the box, including meta tags, clean structure, sitemaps and performance optimisation.

Can I update the website myself?

Absolutely. We build on easy-to-use platforms like WordPress or Shopify (or a custom CMS) and provide a walkthrough so you can edit content, images and products yourself.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes free post-launch support, and we offer monthly maintenance plans covering updates, backups, security monitoring and content changes.

Do you work with clients outside the USA?

Yes — we work with businesses worldwide. We collaborate remotely using video calls, shared project boards and regular progress updates.
