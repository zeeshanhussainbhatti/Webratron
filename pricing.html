FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size of the project. A small business website is much quicker than a custom web portal or e-commerce store. We share a clear timeline with you before work begins.

How much does a website cost?

Our Startup Website Package starts at $469, the Professional package at $1,269 and custom web portals at $5,499. Logo design starts at $149 and video animation at $499. Contact us for a quote tailored to your needs.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Yes. Every site we build is fully responsive, fast-loading and follows SEO best practices out of the box, including meta tags, clean structure, sitemaps and performance optimisation.

Can I update the website myself?

Absolutely. We build on easy-to-use platforms like WordPress or Shopify (or a custom CMS) and provide a walkthrough so you can edit content, images and products yourself.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer website maintenance covering updates, fixes, security and content changes. Ask us about support options for your package.

Do I own the final design?

Yes. Our packages include full ownership rights to your unique design.

Do you work with clients outside the USA?

Yes. We are based in New York and work with clients remotely through calls, email and regular progress updates.
