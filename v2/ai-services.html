What AI services does Webratron offer?

Webratron offers AI Search Optimization (AEO and GEO), AI chatbots, AI voice agents, AI agents and workflow automation, AI content creation, AI video and avatars, AI image and creative design, AI-powered PPC, custom AI apps and integrations, and AI strategy, consulting and training.

What is AI Search Optimization (AEO / GEO)?

AI Search Optimization makes your business more likely to be cited or recommended by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. It combines structured data, llms.txt, answer-first content, FAQs and brand mentions.

How much do AI services cost?

Every AI project is scoped to your goals, so we provide a custom quote after a free AI audit. Contact Webratron for a no-obligation consultation.

Will an AI chatbot or voice agent replace my staff?

No. AI assistants handle repetitive questions, bookings and follow-ups around the clock, and hand complex conversations to your team, so your staff can focus on higher-value work.

Is my business data safe?

Yes. We follow data-privacy best practices, only use the data needed for your project, and can work with providers and settings that do not use your data for model training.

Do I need a new website to use AI services?

No. Our AI chatbots, voice agents, automation and AI search optimization work with your existing website, CRM and tools. We can also build a new site if you need one.
