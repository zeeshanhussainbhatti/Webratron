Shopify vs WordPress: Which Is Right for Your Online Store?

Sep 2026 · 6 min read

Both platforms power millions of stores. Here is how to decide which one suits your products, budget and growth plans.

If you are planning to sell online, two names come up again and again: Shopify and WordPress (with WooCommerce). Both are excellent — but they suit different businesses.

Shopify: simple, hosted and built for selling

  • Hosting, security and updates are handled for you.
  • Fast setup with a polished checkout and built-in payments.
  • Large app store for reviews, upsells, subscriptions and more.
  • Monthly subscription plus transaction or app fees.

WordPress + WooCommerce: flexible and fully yours

  • Complete control over design, features and data.
  • Ideal if content and blogging are a big part of your marketing.
  • Thousands of plugins for almost any feature.
  • You manage hosting, updates and security (or have a partner do it).

Which should you choose?

Choose Shopify if you want to launch quickly, focus on selling and avoid technical maintenance. Choose WordPress with WooCommerce if you want maximum flexibility, a content-heavy site or deeper customisation.
