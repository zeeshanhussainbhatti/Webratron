Still have questions? Call (917) 924-6809 or email support@webratron.com.

Should I build my store on Shopify or WooCommerce?

Choose Shopify for a fast, hosted store with minimal maintenance. Choose WordPress with WooCommerce for maximum flexibility and content-heavy marketing. Read our Shopify vs WordPress guide for a full comparison.

How much does an e-commerce website cost?

Custom e-commerce packages are $669, $1,769, $2,969 and $4,969. Shopify packages are $699, $2,299 and $2,799, depending on products, pages and features.

How many products can my store have?

From up to 10 products on the Basic e-commerce package to 500+ on Business, and unlimited products on the Professional Shopify package.

Do you set up payment gateways?

Yes. We integrate payment gateways and shopping carts so customers can check out securely on any device.
